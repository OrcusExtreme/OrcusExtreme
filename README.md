### 👤 About Me

I am **ORCUS (Jeongmin)**, a researcher and developer dedicated to **Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, and Data Science**. 

Operating under the philosophy of *"Standing on the Border"*, I explore the convergence of rigorous data analytics, neural representation learning, and high-performance software engineering. My work centers on solving complex computational challenges—from training deep neural architectures and building computer vision recognition pipelines to architecting efficient, asynchronous desktop systems.

- 🔬 **Research Lab & Focus**: AI/ML research at MTIL, focusing on Deep Learning models, Material Recognition Technology, and Sensor/Camera Calibration.
- 📊 **Data Science & Analytics**: Big Data competition experience in geospatial classification, feature engineering, and automated spatial intelligence.
- ⚡ **Engineering Principles**: Precision, scalability, reproducible research pipelines, and responsive software design.
